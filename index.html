Dental AI — IOPA Research Assistant

Early-stage research prototype for intraoral periapical radiographs

1. Upload an IOPA image

Use only synthetic, public-domain, or properly de-identified images that you are authorized to use for research. Do not upload identifiable patient records.

For this first version, the image is previewed in your browser only. It is not uploaded to an AI server.

2. Research status

AI model not connected yet.
This version only checks the image file and displays a preview. It does not interpret the X-ray or provide a diagnosis.

Next development stage: connect a documented, IOPA-specific research model through a separate backend, then validate it against dentist-reviewed reference findings.

Safety and privacy

Research use only — not for diagnosis or treatment decisions. Do not use this prototype to rule out disease, delay care, or choose treatment.
  • This page does not save the selected image or send it to a server.
  • Do not upload patient images unless you have the necessary consent, authorization, and institutional approval.
  • Clinical use would require appropriate validation, security, and review of applicable regulatory requirements.
